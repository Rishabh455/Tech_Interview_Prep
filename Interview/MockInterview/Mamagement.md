
## 1. Exact interview-ready answer (English)

I evaluated my junior developers' progress based on three main factors: technical understanding, code quality, and independence in solving problems.

Initially, I provided them with smaller tasks and guided them through the debugging process. I observed how they analyzed the problem, identified the root cause, and implemented the solution.

Over time, I gradually assigned them more challenging tasks and evaluated whether they could solve similar issues independently, write maintainable code, and complete tasks with less guidance.

I also provided regular feedback on their strengths and areas for improvement and shared relevant progress updates with my manager.

My main objective was not just to measure how quickly they completed tasks, but to help them become more confident and independent developers.
