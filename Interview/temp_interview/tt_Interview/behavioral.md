# Infosys Managerial + Behavioral Round: Top 25 Questions

## A. Project ownership and technical decision-making

सबसे ज्यादा priority — managerial interviewer तुम्हारे resume से यहीं से follow-up निकाल सकता है।

1\. Explain your current project and your exact roles and responsibilities.

P0 · Must prepare

तुम्हारी approach: Banking onboarding project का flow, तुम्हारे modules, Java/Spring Boot APIs, PostgreSQL, Angular, external integrations और production support। Team क्या करती है और तुमने personally क्या किया, इसे अलग-अलग बताना।

2\. What is the most challenging problem you have solved in your project?

P0 · Must prepare

तुम्हारी approach: SQL performance optimization या duplicate processing/idempotency में से एक मजबूत वास्तविक incident चुनो। Problem, root cause, तुम्हारा solution और verification बताओ।

3\. Tell me about a production issue you handled under pressure.

P0 · Must prepare

तुम्हारी approach: तुम्हारे पहले से तैयार production incidents में से एक चुनो। Logs, database checks, debugging, root-cause analysis और fix की sequence explain करो।

4\. What was your individual contribution, and how was it different from the rest of your team?

P0 · Must prepare

तुम्हारी approach: तुम्हारे द्वारा implemented APIs, validation, integrations, debugging या optimization के concrete examples दो। Team का पूरा achievement अपने नाम मत लेना।

5\. Why did you choose that particular technical solution? What alternatives did you consider?

P1 · Revise

तुम्हारी approach: किसी वास्तविक decision का उदाहरण दो, जैसे database index, idempotency key, DTO/service layer separation या external API failure handling। बताओ कि solution उस context में क्यों उचित था और उसकी limitations क्या थीं।

## B. Behavioral questions — teamwork, conflict and ownership

6\. Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate or senior. How did you resolve it?

Answer direction: Technical issue पर facts, logs या code evidence के साथ discussion; personal conflict नहीं। अंतिम decision और outcome भी बताना।

7\. What would you do if your manager disagreed with your technical approach?

Answer direction: अपना reasoning और trade-offs समझाओ, feedback सुनो, requirements और team decision का सम्मान करो। यदि security या major reliability risk हो, उसे evidence के साथ escalate करो।

8\. Tell me about a mistake or failure you made and what you learned from it.

P0 · Must prepare

Answer direction: ऐसा वास्तविक उदाहरण जिसमें तुम्हारी learning दिखे। गलती कैसे पहचानी, impact कैसे संभाला और recurrence रोकने के लिए क्या बदला। Blame shifting मत करना।

9\. How do you handle criticism or negative feedback during a code review?

Answer direction: Feedback को personally नहीं लेना, reasoning समझना, code improve करना और जरूरी होने पर technical discussion करना।

10\. Tell me about a time you took ownership of something beyond your assigned task.

P1 · Prepare an example

Answer direction: किसी recurring production issue की root-cause investigation, documentation, testing improvement या preventive fix का genuine example। अपने actual contribution की सीमा स्पष्ट रखना।

## C. Management scenarios — deadlines, pressure and priorities

11\. You have a production incident and a sprint deadline on the same day. How will you prioritize?

P0 · Must prepare

Answer direction: पहले impact, severity और customer/business risk assess करो। High-severity production issue के लिए incident process follow करो, stakeholders को inform करो और sprint commitment की realistic re-planning करो।

12\. What would you do if you realized a committed deadline could not be met?

Answer direction: Risk जल्दी communicate करो, remaining work और blockers बताओ, scope/dependencies पर options दो और revised estimate commit करो। Deadline miss होने के बाद surprise नहीं देना।

13\. How do you manage multiple tasks when all stakeholders say their task is urgent?

Answer direction: Business impact, severity, deadline, dependencies और effort के आधार पर prioritization; manager/PO के साथ alignment और transparent updates।

14\. What would you do if a team member was not delivering their work on time?

Answer direction: पहले privately बात करके blocker समझना, dependencies clear करना और support देना। Milestone impact होने पर manager को timely update करना — सीधे blame नहीं करना।

15\. How do you balance delivery speed with code quality and testing?

Answer direction: Critical validations, unit tests, regression checks और code review को छोड़े बिना delivery optimize करना। Risky shortcuts और unnecessary perfectionism दोनों से बचना।

## D. Communication, client handling and engineering maturity

16\. How would you explain a complex technical problem to a non-technical manager or client?

Answer direction: Technical jargon के बजाय business impact, current status, workaround, risk और next action बताना।

17\. Tell me about a time you worked with distributed teams or multiple stakeholders.

Answer direction: TCS project के वास्तविक coordination का example। Clear written updates, ownership, dependency tracking और handovers की भूमिका समझाओ।

18\. What would you do if the requirements changed just before a release?

Answer direction: Change का impact, testing effort, regression risk और dependencies assess करो। Product owner/manager के साथ scope और release decision align करो; बिना assessment के production change न करो।

19\. How do you ensure that your code is maintainable and production-ready?

Answer direction: SOLID principles जहां relevant हों, clear separation of responsibilities, readable code, meaningful exception handling, unit/integration tests, code reviews और appropriate logging।

20\. Suppose an AWS-hosted application becomes unavailable after deployment. What would you do first?

P0 · Resume-specific

Answer direction: User impact और deployment timeline confirm करना; application/EC2 health, logs, CloudWatch alarms, network/security configuration और recent changes जाँचना। फिर safe rollback या recovery plan, communication और root-cause analysis। अपनी वास्तविक AWS access और experience के अनुरूप answer देना।

## E. Motivation, career maturity and fitment

21\. Why do you want to leave TCS, and why are you interested in Infosys?

P0 · Must prepare

Answer direction: Positive growth-oriented reason — broader engineering challenges, backend/cloud exposure, larger responsibilities। TCS या manager की बुराई नहीं करना। Infosys के लिए role से संबंधित genuine reason देना।

22\. Why should we hire you for a Senior Developer role with just over three years of experience?

P0 · Must prepare

Answer direction: Practical development, secure REST APIs, production troubleshooting, database optimization, full-stack understanding और responsibility लेने के examples। Seniority का दावा सिर्फ years पर नहीं, demonstrated ownership पर रखना।

23\. What are your strengths and one area you are working to improve?

Answer direction: Strengths के लिए concrete examples चुनो — problem-solving, debugging, ownership या learning agility। Improvement area वास्तविक हो और उसके लिए तुम क्या कर रहे हो, यह भी बताना।

24\. How do you learn a new technology or handle a task you have never done before?

Answer direction: Documentation, small proof of concept, testing, code review और team feedback। AWS question के संदर्भ में साफ बताना कि क्या खुद configure किया है और क्या सिर्फ support या observe किया है।

25\. Where do you see yourself in three years, and what would you contribute to our team?

Answer direction: Strong backend/full-stack engineer बनना, cloud architecture और production reliability में depth बढ़ाना, code quality और team outcomes में योगदान देना। केवल designation या promotion पर focus मत करना।

## 7. Answers कैसे prepare करने हैं?

Behavioral answers के लिए STAR framework इस्तेमाल करो:

- S — Situation: क्या हुआ था?
- T — Task: तुम्हारी responsibility क्या थी?
- A — Action: तुमने personally क्या किया?
- R — Result: क्या outcome निकला और क्या सीखा?

प्रत्येक कहानी लगभग 60–90 seconds में explain करने की practice करो। जहाँ possible हो, result को verifiable रखना; कोई metric या achievement invent नहीं करना।

मेरी recommendation: इन 25 में से पहले Questions 1, 2, 3, 6, 8, 11, 12, 20, 21 और 22 तैयार करो। ये तुम्हारी project credibility, ownership, pressure handling, AWS exposure और Infosys के साथ fitment को cover करते हैं।

Interview के अंत में interviewer से एक thoughtful सवाल भी पूछना: “What would be the key expectations from the person joining this role in the first three to six months?” इससे role की expectations समझने और अपनी maturity दिखाने, दोनों में मदद मिलेगी।
